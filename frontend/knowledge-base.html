Problem: Want to return product

Solution: Go to "My Orders", click "Return". Print label and send back. Refund in 5-7 business days after inspection.

refund

Problem: Return processed but refund not received

Solution: Refunds take 5-7 days after inspection plus 1-3 days bank processing. Contact support if over 10 days.

Problem: Card declined at checkout

Problem: Can't log in

Solution: Click "Forgot Password" on login page. Check email for reset link within 2 minutes. Check spam folder if not received.

Technical Issues

Website Not Loading

Problem: Website slow or app crashes

Solution: Clear cache, try different browser, disable extensions, restart device, check connection. For app: update, uninstall/reinstall, check storage.

technical

Checkout Stuck

Problem: Can't complete purchase

Solution: Disable ad blockers, clear cache, use incognito mode, try different browser, check connection. Contact support with cart contents if stuck.
